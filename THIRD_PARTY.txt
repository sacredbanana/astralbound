Astronomical catalog:
  data/stars.csv and src/star_catalog.inc adapt David Nash's HYG v4.1 database
  (Hipparcos/Yale/Gliese), https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database .
  These adapted data are CC BY-SA 4.0, not Unlicense. Attribution, source IDs,
  changes and reproducible import instructions: data/README.md.
  License text: data/LICENSE-HYG.txt; https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .
  Star names are the IAU Catalog of Star Names (IAU Working Group on Star
  Names, https://www.iau.org/public/themes/naming_stars/), CC BY.
  Stars outside Hipparcos are placed from SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) and the
  NASA Exoplanet Archive (Caltech/IPAC); each row of data/extra-stars.csv
  names its source.
  Solar System elements/physical parameters are from NASA/JPL Solar System
  Dynamics; see data/README.md for sources, snapshot and approximation limits.
  Solar System surface and cloud-top maps (data/maps/, src/body_maps.inc) are
  reduced from NOAA ETOPO5; NASA Blue Marble (NASA Earth Observatory, Reto
  Stoeckli); MOLA, TES, LOLA and MESSENGER products via PDS and USGS
  Astrogeology; the NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit (Ernie Wright); Cassini's Jupiter map
  (NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute); Galileo, Voyager and New Horizons mosaics
  (USGS Astrogeology); and Hubble OPAL maps (NASA/ESA HST, OPAL program, PI:
  Simon, GO13937, archived by STScI, doi:10.17909/T9G593). Sources, processing
  and credits: data/README.md.
